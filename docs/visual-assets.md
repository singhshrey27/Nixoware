### Common brief

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: standalone illustration for NIXOWARE's existing teal/cyan technology website. Primary request: replace repetitive lower-quality desk mockups with a striking, sophisticated photorealistic premium technology scene. Generate a crisp high-resolution landscape 2560 by 1706 image (roughly 3:2), with detailed materials and straight, optically sharp device edges. Match brand palette dark teal #04364a, cyan #12dced, pale #e8f6f8, brushed silver, charcoal and white. Art direction: high-end commercial product photography, elegant architectural studio lighting, realistic glass, anodized aluminium, refined cyan edge accents, deep but clean shadows, deliberate uncluttered composition. This is illustrative marketing imagery, NEVER a client screenshot or evidence of business results. Do not make UI screenshot layouts as the entire output; render a genuine physical scene. Screens have tasteful simple coherent UI with only a few short accurate labels, no fabricated statistics, percentage counters, invented client names, testimonials, tiny gibberish, or excessive fake text. Buttons inside device screens must be cyan, not orange/red/pink. No watermarks, no external logos. No text floating over the photograph. Main subject fills the center-right 65%; keep the left 20% quiet, matching teal atmosphere, so existing native website copy and masks can coexist. Keep all device corners inside frame and focus consistent across foreground subjects; shallow focus only in the background. Photography must read clearly at small card sizes and in mobile crops.
